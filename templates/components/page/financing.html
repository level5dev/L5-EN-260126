{{#if (lang (concat (concat 'custom.page.financing.financing_partner.head-'$index)'.id')) '==' 'credit--key'}}
Credit Key is the perfect option for businesses that would like to start a line of credit. Applying does not impact your personal credit, and you can be approved for up to $50k. Note: Credit Key is only available for orders larger than $1500 and requires an EIN number. Here's a step-by-step guide for applying.

1. Head to checkout - Once you've picked out your tool set or individual tools, head to the LEVEL5 checkout and fill in your shipping & billing information. At the Payment section, select Credit Key as your payment method. You should see something like this:


2. Click “Continue with Credit Key” - After you've clicked the button, you will be redirected to the Credit Key application. Here you will need to fill in your business & contact information. Please note you will need to supply an EIN number for this application (if you don't have one, try PayPal Pay Monthly instead). The application should look something like this:


3. Receive Approval - Credit Key will provide you with an answer to your submission right away so you can continue your purchase at checkout. Upon approval, you can head back to the LEVEL5 checkout and complete your purchase!


4. Enjoy Your Tools - We ship as soon as we have confirmation of financing from Credit Key. Our team will deliver your tools within 3 - 5 business days. You receive all of the benefits of ownership like LEVEL5's unmatched warranties and customer support!

Managing Your Payments


All of your payments will be made & managed through the Credit Key app. You can log in here to view your payment information: https://borrower.creditkey.com/login. If you require additional tools, you can use your Credit Key line of credit to make additional purchases within your approved credit amount.

{{else if (lang (concat (concat 'custom.page.financing.financing_partner.head-'$index)'.id')) '==' 'paypal--monthly'}}
PayPal Pay Monthly is a great option for individuals looking for a simple way to finance a tool set. This option is designed for orders over $1500 and works with your existing PayPal account.

1. Head to checkout - Once you've picked out your tool set or individual tools, head to the LEVEL5 checkout and fill in your shipping & billing information. At the Payment section, select PayPal Pay Later as your payment method (Note: Your order must be over $1500 to see this option). It will look something like this:


2. Click “Continue with Pay Later” - After you've click the button, you'll be prompted to sign up to PayPal or log in to your existing account. You will be taken through the application process where you will need to provide some personal information, contact details, Social Security Number or Individual Tax Identification Number, date of birth, and annual gross income.


3. Receive Approval - PayPal will provide your with a decision in seconds, at which point you can complete your purchase at the LEVEL5 checkout.


4. Enjoy Your Tools - We ship as soon as we have confirmation of financing from PayPal. Our team will deliver your tools within 3 - 5 business days. You receive all of the benefits of ownership like LEVEL5's unmatched warranties and customer support!

Managing Your Payments


Using your PayPal account, you can manage all of your payments easily with PayPal's Autopay feature.

{{else if (lang (concat (concat 'custom.page.financing.financing_partner.head-'$index)'.id')) '==' 'klarna--'}}
Klarna is a service that lets customers split up their payments into 4 payments separated by 2 week intervals with no interest. There's no fees or interest when you pay on time.

1. Head to checkout - Once you've added products to your cart (Note: Klarna is only available for orders between $50 and $1500) head to the LEVEL5 checkout and fill in your shipping & billing information. At the Payment section, select Klarna as your payment method. You should see something like this:


2. Click “Place Order” - After you've clicked the button, a Klarna popup will guide you through the application process and provide you with an instant approval decision. You only need to provide basic information, so you can complete your purchase with full transparency.


3. Receive Approval - Once you've received an approval decision, you can complete your purchase through the LEVEL5 checkout.


4. Enjoy Your Tools - We ship as soon as we have confirmation of financing from Klarna. Our team will deliver your tools within 3 - 5 business days. You receive all of the benefits of ownership like LEVEL5's unmatched warranties and customer support!

Managing Your Payments


You can view all of your balances and remaining payments via the Klarna app or through the website.

*Monthly financing through Klarna issued by WebBank. Other CA resident loans made or arranged pursuant to a California Financing Law license. NMLS # 1353190.

3. Enjoy Your Tools - We ship as soon as we have confirmation of financing from PayPal. Our team will deliver your tools within 3 - 5 business days. You receive all of the benefits of ownership like LEVEL5's unmatched warranties and customer support!

Managing Your Payments


Using your PayPal account, you can manage all of your payments easily with PayPal's Autopay feature.
