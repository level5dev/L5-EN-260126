Adult signature required
For your protection, the "Adult Signature Required" option ensures your order is securely delivered and not left unattended.

All orders over $250 default to "Adult Signature Required." We strongly recommend keeping this option enabled. However, if you live in a secure or remote area and prefer delivery without a signature, you may choose to disable this option.
