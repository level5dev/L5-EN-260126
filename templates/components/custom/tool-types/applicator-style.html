CORNERS CONQUERED.

Your choice in corner applicator tools comes down to convenience, preference, and job type. Here’s a breakdown of the key differences:

Corner Applicator: A 7” all-aluminum, corrosion resistant housing featuring a proprietary urethane wiper seal. The key benefits are:

  • Lightweight on the wall
  • Manual mud control for those who want it
  • Lower upfront cost
  • Easy to clean & maintain

MiniShot: A pressurized compound applicator that delivers mud without the manual pushing of a backplate. During the filling process, back pressure is created that seamlessly dispenses mud with an adjustable flow when pressed onto the wall. The key benefits are:

  • Very fast and smooth operation for larger projects
  • No pushing on the wall, when the Minishot is activated the flow nozzle ensures consistent coverage.
  • Holds significantly more mud, meaning fewer trips back and forth to the pump and longer runs across the wall.
  • Less fatigue over long days.
